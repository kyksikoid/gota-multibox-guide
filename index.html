Solo-Trick Tutorial Multibox Gameplay
This feature is built specifically for multibox play across two tabs with one primary large cell and one smaller feeder cell. On your primary cell, direct your mouse toward the attack trajectory and hold Freeze Mouse (or Line + Freeze Mouse) to lock its movement vector. Next, switch to your feeder cell, steer toward the action, and trigger the trick bind corresponding to your mass—for example, at approximately 20k mass, tap default key 7 (Trick 128). Upon pressing the bind, your active feeder cell instantly splits 7 times (into 128 pieces) toward your cursor, while your primary cell in the background simultaneously fires 5 splits (32 pieces) along the locked line, waits for the configured Solo Delay (default 800ms in the top slider), and automatically splits another 5 times (32 pieces) to feed forward and finish the trick, achieving an automated independent trick without manual tab switching.
