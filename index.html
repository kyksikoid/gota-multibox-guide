Solo-Trick (Multibox Trick)

01
Lock Primary (Big) Cell Vector
Aim your cursor toward the target trajectory and hold Freeze Mouse (or the combination of Line + Freeze Mouse). This locks the travel vector for your primary account's automated splits.
02
Direct Secondary (Small) Cell
Keep your active browser tab on the smaller feeder account. Move toward the enemy cell and point your cursor precisely where the split mass needs to land.
03
Trigger Mass-Specific Bind
Press your configured keybind based on your small cell's current mass. For instance, at approximately 20k mass, press the default key 7 (Trick 128 — dividing into 128 pieces through 7 rapid splits).
04
Automated Synchronous Split Cycle
Pressing the bind executes two synchronized operations across both tabs:
1. The active small cell instantly splits toward your cursor (7 splits).
2. The background big cell performs 5 splits (32 pieces) along the frozen direction, pauses for the exact configured delay (Solo Delay, default 800ms), and performs an additional 5 splits (32 pieces) to complete the trick and feed forward.

Result: An independent, instantaneous multibox trick executed entirely from your feeder tab without manual window switching.
