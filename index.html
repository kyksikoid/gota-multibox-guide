Solo-Trick

This feature is made strictly for playing with a multibox setup across two tabs, where you have one big cell and one small feeder. On your big cell, point your cursor in the direction you want to shoot and toggle Freeze Mouse (or toggle Line + Freeze Mouse). Do not hold it down, you must toggle it on or it will not trigger.

Next, switch to your small cell, push toward the fight, and tap the trick key that fits your current mass. For example, if you have around 20k mass, tap the default key 7 (Trick 128). Your small cell instantly splits 7 times (into 128 pieces) toward your cursor. At the exact same moment, the big cell in the background fires 5 splits (32 pieces) along your locked line, waits for your configured Solo Delay (800ms by default in the top slider), and fires another 5 splits (32 pieces).

Right after tapping your trick key, all you have to do is hold your standard in-game feed key so your feeder cell dumps its mass properly. That gives you a clean, independent solo-trick without ever switching tabs manually.

Fast-Feed

Want to quickly dump mass into yourself or feed a teammate? That is what Fast-Feed is for. Toggle Freeze Mouse in the direction you want to feed, then hit your Fast-Feed bind. Only the tab you pressed the bind from will split.

It executes 5 splits (32 pieces), pauses for your configured delay, and then shoots another 5 splits (32 pieces). P.S. when you are sitting on heavy mass (like 800k), bump your delay slider up slightly to around 850ms so it does not trigger too early.

Auto-Rev

Look, I know Auto-Rev is not built the cleanest way, but we had no other choice because the game is heavily protected. For this feature to actually work well, you need decent FPS and low ping, plus you have to strictly follow these rules:

The target cell must be fully visible and not hidden behind other cells or screen edges. You MUST disable mass display, skins, and enemy nicknames in your game settings, or the script will instantly fail and cause false triggers. Keep your camera completely static and avoid zooming during use.

Note: There is a ~10ms processing delay with a slight margin of error, but it is built this way to stay working across future game updates. You will get used to it.
